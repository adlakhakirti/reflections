## Whole-view findings

The cases are isolated synthetic scenarios, not a complete quarterly reflection about one person. Whole-view fairness across a realistic longitudinal corpus is NOT TESTED. No system-level retrieval was run, so counterexample recall is NOT TESTED. Permission controls, persistence, deletion, coaching outcomes, and model regressions are NOT TESTED.

## Product implications

Validate references and counts before displaying claims. Independently review context and scope even when source checks pass. Preserve the distinction between source integrity and semantic support in any dashboard or article. Do not present the six failed fixture cases as a model failure rate.
